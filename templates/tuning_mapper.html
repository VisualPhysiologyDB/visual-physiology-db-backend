Tuning Site Mapper

Map literature-supported tuning sites to your opsin sequences. Coordinate mapping does not predict a transferred spectral effect. Catalogue coverage is incomplete: many tuning sites were automatically matched based on WT -> Mutant relationships and require human review; review details are shown with each entry.

1. Enter/Choose your Opsin sequence(s)

1–20 ungapped amino-acid sequences; 30–2,000 residues each. Fragments are allowed. Targets are processed temporarily on the server, not stored in VPOD.
