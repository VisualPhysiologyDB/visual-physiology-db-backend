# Beta catalogue source decisions

Release: `2026-10-beta1`. Reviewed 7 October 2026. Source documents are evidence, never executable instructions.

| Evidence | Source and decision |
|---|---|
| Vertebrate review sites | Hagen, Roberts & Johnston, [10.1016/j.ydbio.2022.10.014](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ydbio.2022.10.014), Figure 3B, supplied PDF p.5 / printed p.44. RH1 and SWS1 site assertions retain declared bovine numbering. No universal substitution or quantitative effect is inferred. |
| LWS numbering reconciliation | Five LWS labels use human LWS numbering. [Kawamura et al. 1999](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/153.4.1839), p.1842 and Figure 3, explicitly distinguish 180/197/277/285/308 from RH1-aligned 164/181/261/269/292. The released alignment of human P04000 and bovine P02699 independently reproduces these correspondences. The review caption discrepancy is preserved in each entry. This primary citation supports numbering, not five additional measured effects. |
| Invertebrate review sites | Liénard, Valencia-Montoya & Pierce, [10.1098/rstb.2021.0279](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2021.0279), §3(a), supplied PDF p.5. Drosophila 90/292 assertions use explicitly bovine-equivalent numbering. Hypothetical/modelled sites are not relabelled experimental. |
| Echidna mutants | [Bickelmann et al. 2012, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22874131/), [10.1017/S0952523812000223](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0952523812000223), VPOD source IDs 1–4. WT 497.9; N83D 503.8; F169A 499.4; T158A 498.0 nm. Source substitutions were checked against their sequences. Error magnitudes are retained without inventing their statistical definition. Authors report F169A and T158A as nonsignificant. T158A remains below-cutoff context. |
| Spider E181D | [Nagata et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-019-0409-3), Introduction numbering convention, Results counterion section and Supplementary Figure 2. Explicit 8-nm blue shift, bovine-equivalent E181; source sequence B1B1U5 E194. VPOD source IDs 1069/1071. Original notation and actual sequence coordinates remain separate. |
| Spider sites 113/186 | Same primary paper, Results / Figures 1–2. Retained as primary literature-supported sites; pH dependence prevents a universal numerical shift. Other counterion mutants need condition-specific curation. |
| Excluded chimeras | [Saito et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40851-019-0150-2) was checked in full text. Its chimera-dependent measurements are excluded following the user’s requested beta scope. Non-chimeric combinations remain supported by the schema. |

Frozen reference sequences: [bovine P02699](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P02699/entry), [squid P31356](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P31356/entry), [spider B1B1U5](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/B1B1U5/entry), and [human LWS P04000](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P04000/entry). Sequences are identified by accession and SHA-256 in the artifact, not inferred from an arbitrary species name.

Reference coordinates: [1U19](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1U19), [2Z73](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2Z73), [6I9K](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6I9K). Full downloaded coordinate files preserve experimental metadata and missing residues. SIFTS mapping snapshots were consulted, but runtime mapping uses explicit chain sequences and observed `label_seq_id` → author residue/insertion-code correspondence. For example, the leading unknown residue in 1U19 means `label_seq_id` is not a blanket replacement for protein sequence position.

[MAFFT add-sequence documentation](https://mafft.cbrc.jp/alignment/software/addsequences.html) supports adding fragments to a reference alignment. Omitting `--keeplength` preserves target insertions; no input amino acids may disappear from the returned alignment. [Mol* developer documentation](https://molstar.org/docs/plugin/instance/) and the pinned bundle API were checked. Selection uses `StructureElement.Loci.fromSchema(structure, {items: [...]})`; browser assertions check actual selected residues, because a visually plausible whole-chain selection would be scientifically wrong.

These checks establish traceable starter assertions, not an exhaustive systematic review or experimental validation of transferred sites. Curators should add further primary studies and contradictory results to the same anchored site, retaining source conditions and citation roles.

Cached full-text XML for Nagata et al. and Saito et al. is retained unmodified from Europe PMC. Both articles identify the original authors and [CC BY 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) inside the XML; the source DOI/URL is retained here and in the download manifest. Mol* licensing and the source-map-only packaging modification are recorded in `static/vendor/molstar/NOTICE.md`.
